# Bywater BASIC Interpreter

The Bywater BASIC Interpreter (bwBASIC) implements a large superset of the ANSI Standard for Minimal BASIC (X3.60-1978) and a significant subset of the ANSI Standard for Full BASIC (X3.113-1987) in C.

- [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/projects/bwbasic/)
- [BwBasic on GitHub](https://github.com/kenmartin-unix/BwBasic)
- [Bwbasic-3.20b on GitHub](https://github.com/kenmartin-unix/Bwbasic-3.20b)
- [Bwbasic-3.20b-for-BeagleBone on GitHub](https://github.com/kenmartin-unix/Bwbasic-3.2a-for-BeagleBone)
